From our conversation

What we heard from your team.

Managing delivery across 20–50 KFC locations — across three platforms — creates a level of operational complexity that standard tools weren't designed for.

📊

Fragmented data, no unified view

Glovo, Bolt Food, and Wolt each have their own dashboards, reporting formats, and data logic. Getting a consolidated view of what's happening across all locations requires manual exports, cross-referencing spreadsheets, and hours of work — every single week.

~1 full day/week lost to manual reporting
🍔

Menu updates that don't scale

A single menu change — a price update, a product swap, an availability flag — requires logging into each platform separately. Across 20–50 locations and three platforms, inconsistencies accumulate. Errors happen. And the team that should be focused on performance is doing maintenance.

Inconsistencies across platforms compound over time
📣

Campaigns running without feedback

Promotions go live across platforms but tracking their actual impact — by location, by channel, by time period — requires manual work that rarely happens fast enough to adjust. Budget gets spent without clear visibility into what's working and what isn't.

Marketing spend without ROI clarity
🔍

Limited visibility into market position

At your scale, results don't just depend on what you do internally — they depend on how the market moves around you. Without real-time competitor data on pricing, promotions, and positioning, you're reacting rather than leading.

Reactive decisions instead of proactive ones
